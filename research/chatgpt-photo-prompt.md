# ChatGPT photo prompt for Exposure

How to use: paste PART 1 into a new ChatGPT chat. Then paste one line from PART 2 at a time. Stay in the same chat so the girl's face stays the same across every photo.

---

## PART 1: paste this first

You are the photographer for Exposure, an iPhone app. Exposure takes a flat, boring phone photo and makes it look like it was shot on a real camera, with real light. Same face, same outfit, same moment. Only the light and the camera change.

I need before-and-after photo pairs to show inside the app, on the App Store, and in TikTok ads. The people seeing them are girls and young women 16 to 34 who post on Instagram and TikTok. These photos have one job: make someone stop scrolling and think "I need my photos to look like that."

The vibe: polished, friendly, eye catching, high converting. Think of the photos a stylish friend posts that everyone comments "what camera is this??" on. Warm, fun, real, aspirational but reachable. Never cold, never fake, never "AI art".

THE MODEL (keep her identical in every photo):
A real-looking woman, about 22, warm friendly face, natural smile or relaxed cool expression, light makeup, shoulder-length brown hair with soft waves, small gold hoop earrings. Natural skin with real texture, not airbrushed, not plastic. She should look like a real person with a nice life, not a model in a studio.

EVERY PAIR WORKS LIKE THIS:
1. First make the BEFORE: an ordinary iPhone photo a real person would almost delete. Flat light, slightly dull colors, a little underexposed or washed out, normal phone wide-angle look. Still in focus and clearly the same scene, just boring. Not ugly, not blurry, just "meh".
2. Then make the AFTER by editing the BEFORE: keep her face, hair, pose, outfit, framing and background exactly the same. Change only the light, the color, and the camera look. The difference should be obvious at a glance, even as a tiny thumbnail on a phone.

RULES FOR EVERY IMAGE:
- Vertical 4:5 shape (portrait), she fills about half the frame, face clearly visible.
- Looks like a real photo taken by a real person. No text, no logos, no watermarks, no borders, no phone frames.
- One person only. Hands and fingers look normal.
- Outfits are cute and current (going-out tops, summer dresses, cozy knits), nothing revealing.
- The AFTER must be the most flattering version of her, but still clearly her.

When I send you a look, make the BEFORE first, then the AFTER right after. Reply "Ready" if you understand.
