## Description
Exposure turns the phone photo you already want to post into the shot a real camera would have taken. Same face, same outfit, same moment. Only the light and the camera change.

Pick a photo, pick a look, and Exposure rebuilds it in about 30 seconds.

LOOKS
• Flash: night flash, daylight flash, red flash and paparazzi
• Digicam: early-2000s compact cameras and point-and-shoot glow
• Film: warm and cool consumer film, plus classic 35mm
• Light: golden hour, blue hour, soft window light and a gentle glow
• Booth: four-frame photobooth strips and an old bedroom webcam

MAKE IT YOURS
Every result opens in the editor. Dial the look from subtle to full, then adjust warmth, brightness, contrast, grain and vignette before you save or share.

FREE PHOTOBOOTH
The Studio is a real photobooth. It counts down, shoots a strip of frames and prints it on white or black paper. It runs entirely on your phone and is free to use.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account needed. Your gallery lives on your phone. Looks are made with Google's Gemini AI, and Exposure asks before any photo leaves your device. Photos are never stored on our servers.

PLANS
Exposure Weekly gives you 5 photos a day and Exposure Yearly gives you 10 a day. Unused photos bank for up to three days. Photo packs are also available and never expire.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Manage or cancel any time in your Apple Account settings.

### Notes
No account or sign-in is needed.

HOW TO TEST THE AI LOOKS
1. Swipe through the four intro slides and tap "Choose a photo". Pick any photo with a person in it.
2. Pick a look. Night Flash or Golden Hour show the effect most clearly.
3. Before the first upload, the app asks permission to send the photo to Google Gemini. Tap "Agree and continue".
4. The result arrives in about 30 seconds and appears blurred behind the plan sheet. Exposure uses a hard paywall: a plan or a photo pack unlocks it. Subscribe with a sandbox account to reveal, save and share it.

PURCHASES
Plans (Weekly, Yearly) and photo packs (10, 30, 80) can be bought with a sandbox account from the plan sheet or when photos run out. Restore Purchases is in Settings, the gear icon on the Looks tab.

FREE FEATURES
The Studio tab is an on-device photobooth with countdown and photo strips. It needs no purchase and no network.

AI AND PRIVACY
Looks are generated by Google's Gemini API through our own backend. The app shows a consent screen naming Google Gemini before any photo is sent, and the choice can be turned off in Settings. Photos are processed only to make the requested image and are not stored on our servers. The model keeps the person and scene as they are and changes only lighting and camera style. Gemini's built-in safety filters reject unsafe inputs.
