## Description
Exposure turns your camera roll into a roll of film.

Choose a stock, add up to 12 photos from a night out or a trip, and Exposure develops them together on that one camera. You get back a set, with a contact sheet for the whole roll.

ROLLS, NOT FILTERS
Every frame is re-rendered by AI as that camera would have shot it. The first frame develops first, and the rest are rendered against it so the set belongs together.

YOUR PHOTOS STAY YOURS
Each frame is checked against your original. If something was added or moved, Exposure tries again or falls back to a safe render, so people and places stay as you shot them. Hold any frame to see the original, and set the strength yourself.

ELEVEN STOCKS
Flash: Night Out, Noon Flash, Red Room
Compact cameras: Pocket Compact, 2003 Compact
Film: Sunny 200, Vibrant 400, Shoebox 35
Light: Last Light, After Sunset, North Window

REAL PRINTS
Order your roll as 4x6 prints, printed by our lab and mailed to your door. US only for now.

TRY IT FIRST
Explore a finished sample roll, and develop your first frame free.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account needed. Your rolls live on your phone. Frames are developed with Google's Gemini AI, and Exposure asks before any photo leaves your device.

PLANS
Exposure Weekly gives you 5 photos a day and Exposure Yearly gives you 10 a day. Unused photos bank for up to three days. Photo packs are also available and never expire. Prints are paid separately.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Manage or cancel any time in your Apple Account settings.

## App Review notes
No account or sign-in is needed.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE REJECTION
This is a new concept. We removed single-photo looks, the photobooth and the filter editor. Exposure now develops a whole roll of a user's photos on one camera, delivers a contact sheet, and can mail real prints.

HOW TO TEST
1. Swipe through the three intro screens and tap "Look at a sample roll". This finished roll needs no purchase. Tap a frame and hold it to see the original.
2. Go back, tap "Start a new roll", choose a stock and add 4 or more photos. Tap Develop.
3. Exposure asks permission to send photos to Google Gemini. Tap "Agree and continue".
4. The first frame develops free in about 30 seconds. Remaining frames wait for a plan or pack; use a sandbox account and they develop on the same screen.
5. On a finished roll: Order prints, Save all and Contact sheet.

PRINTS
Prints are physical goods, so payment uses Stripe Checkout rather than in-app purchase (guideline 3.1.3(e)). Test card: (fill in when Stripe is live).

AI AND PRIVACY
Frames are generated by Google's Gemini API through our backend. A consent screen naming Google Gemini appears before any photo is sent and can be turned off in Settings. Photos are not stored on our servers, except frames in a print order, which are deleted within 21 days.
