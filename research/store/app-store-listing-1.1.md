## Description
Exposure turns your camera roll into a roll of film.

Choose a look, add up to 12 photos from a night out or a trip, and Exposure develops them together on that one camera. You get back a matching set, and you can dial the look up or down for the whole roll.

A SET, NOT A SINGLE PHOTO
Frames are rendered by AI as that camera would have shot them. The first frame develops first, and the rest are rendered against it so the set belongs together.

YOUR PHOTOS STAY YOURS
Each frame is checked against your original. If something was added or moved, Exposure tries again, and if it still can't pass it returns a simple render of your original at no charge, so people and places stay as you shot them. Hold any frame to see the original, and slide the strength of the look from subtle to full, for one frame or the whole roll.

TEN LOOKS
Flash: Night Out, Noon Flash, Red Room
Compact cameras: Pocket Compact, 2003 Compact
Film: Sunny 200, Vibrant 400, Shoebox 35
Light: Last Light, After Sunset

REAL PRINTS
Order your roll as 4x6 prints on real silver-halide photo paper, mailed to your door. US only for now.

TRY IT FIRST
Your first frame develops for free on your own photo. A plan or pack unlocks it and develops the rest.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account needed. Your rolls live on your phone. Frames are developed with Google's Gemini AI, and Exposure asks before any photo leaves your device.

PLANS
Exposure Weekly gives you 5 frames a day and Exposure Yearly gives you 10 a day. Unused frames bank for up to three days. One-time packs are also available and never expire. Prints are paid separately.

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Manage or cancel any time in your Apple Account settings.

## App Review notes
No account or sign-in is needed.

WHAT CHANGED SINCE THE REJECTION
This is a new concept and a new app, not an adjustment. We removed single-photo looks, the photobooth and the filter editor. Exposure now develops a whole roll of a user's own photos on one camera look, lets them dial the look up or down, and can mail real prints. The visual identity, icon, copy and onboarding are all new.

HOW TO TEST
1. Onboarding: tap "See how it works", then "Try it on my photo".
2. "Try a look" opens. Tap "Choose a photo" and pick one photo from the library (new users start with one photo).
3. Pick one of the four looks shown (for example "Shoebox 35"), then tap "Develop my photo".
4. Exposure asks permission to send the photo to Google Gemini. Tap "Allow and develop".
5. The photo develops full screen in about 30 seconds. The finished result shows sharp for two seconds, then frosts over and the plans sheet rises. Tap "Unlock Exposure" and complete the purchase with a sandbox account. The frost lifts and the photo is yours to save and share.
6. Now a paid user: on Rolls tap "New roll", add 4 or more photos, and tap "Develop N photos". Each photo sharpens in the grid as it finishes. Tap any photo to swipe through the set, hold it to see the original, and use the Strength slider. The Looks tab lists all ten looks; "Start a roll on <look>" starts a roll with that look.

PRINTS
Prints are physical goods, so payment uses Stripe Checkout rather than in-app purchase (guideline 3.1.3(e)). Stripe is in live mode, so a card would really be charged. To review the flow, tap "Order prints" on a finished roll and go as far as the Stripe page; no payment is needed. Prints ship to the US only.

AI AND PRIVACY
Photos are developed by Google's Gemini API through our backend. A consent screen naming Google Gemini appears before any photo is sent and can be turned off in Settings. Photos are not stored on our servers, except the copies in a print order, which are deleted within 21 days.
