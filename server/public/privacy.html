Exposure — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Legal entity / controller: Tarek Moumane, an individual developer ("Exposure", "we", "us"). Contact: tarekmoumane@gmail.com

Exposure is a mobile app that restyles your photos. It has two kinds of features:

  • Studio and Filters: filters, photobooth strips and adjustments that run entirely on your device. No AI, nothing is transmitted.
  • Looks: AI-generated relighting and camera looks. Your selected photo is sent to our backend, which forwards it to our AI provider to produce the result.

What we are (and aren't) doing

  • No account. You can use Exposure without creating an account or sharing contact details.
  • Studio and Filters stay on your device. Photos edited there are never sent to us or to any provider.
  • Looks use an AI provider. The app sends your selected photo and the chosen look to our backend, which forwards the minimum necessary inputs to Google LLC (Gemini API) to generate the output.
  • Transient processing. Your photos, prompts and outputs are used only to fulfil your request and are not retained on our servers after it completes.
  • Your gallery lives on your device until you delete it.
  • Billing is handled by Apple and Google, managed through RevenueCat. We never see or store payment card data.

Information we collect

  1. Content you choose to provide. Photos you select for a Look are transmitted securely to our backend only to call the AI model and return the result. They are processed transiently and not retained. Google LLC (Gemini API) may retain inputs and outputs for a limited period under its own terms, for example for abuse monitoring.
  2. Subscription and credit signals. Signed transaction data and the original transaction ID from Apple or Google, an anonymous app-generated user identifier used by RevenueCat, and your daily credit and pack balances, to operate paid features and daily limits. These are not used to identify you personally.
  3. Device and network metadata (minimal). Standard request data (IP address, user agent, timestamps) processed temporarily by our hosting provider for security and abuse prevention, and an application token that confirms requests come from the Exposure app.
  4. Analytics and diagnostics. Exposure does not run third-party analytics or crash reporting. Apple and Google may provide us aggregated, anonymous install and crash statistics through their developer tools under their own policies. We never receive your photos through these channels.

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, precise location or payment card details. If you email us, we receive your email address and message.

Face data

Photos you select may contain faces. Exposure does not perform facial recognition, identity matching or biometric analysis, and does not create or store face templates. Photos containing faces are used only to create the image you requested, are shared only with Google LLC (Gemini API) for that purpose, are not stored on our servers after the request completes, and remain on your device until you delete them. We do not use your photos for any purpose other than the image-creation functionality described here.

How we use information

To provide the service, manage subscriptions and credits, prevent abuse, fix bugs and improve the app, and respond to support requests. We do not use your photos, prompts or outputs to train any model.

Third-party processors

Google LLC (Gemini API) (AI image generation), Vercel, Inc. (backend hosting), Upstash, Inc. (storage of anonymous photo balances), RevenueCat, Inc. (subscriptions and entitlements), Apple (StoreKit) and Google (Play Billing). We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Children

Exposure is not directed to children. Minimum age: 13 in the US, 16 in the EU/UK, or higher where required. Do not upload images of minors without verifiable parental consent.

International transfers, retention, security

Our infrastructure operates primarily in the United States. For EU/UK users we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses. Photos, prompts and outputs are not stored server-side after we return your result. Subscription data is retained as long as needed to operate your plan, and transaction records for legally required periods. We use HTTPS, application tokens and minimal retention.

Your rights

Depending on your region you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability by emailing tarekmoumane@gmail.com. Because there are no accounts, we may verify requests via your original transaction ID or anonymous app identifier. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. We do not sell or share your information for cross-context behavioural advertising, do not use advertising SDKs, and do not request App Tracking Transparency permission because we do not track you across apps.

Changes

We will update the "Last updated" date and notify users of material changes.
