Exposure Support

Questions, a frame that didn't come out right, a print order or a billing problem? Email tarekmoumane@gmail.com and you'll get a reply within two working days.

Common questions

A frame failed. Was I charged?

Open the roll and try the frame again. You won't be charged twice for the same frame.

How do daily frames work?

Your first frame develops for free and shows as a blurred preview. A plan or pack unlocks it so you can see, save and share it. Weekly gives you 5 frames a day and Yearly gives you 10. New frames arrive at midnight, and unused ones bank for up to three days. Packs never expire and are used after your daily frames.

Where are my prints?

Open the Prints tab to see your order and its tracking link once it ships. Prints currently ship to the US.

My prints arrived damaged or never arrived

Email us within 30 days with your order and a photo of the problem. We will reprint or refund them.

I reinstalled the app or got a new phone

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Your plan and any unused packs come back. Your rolls are stored only on your phone, so they are not restored.

How do I cancel?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Exposure. You can also tap Manage subscription inside Exposure's Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

Refunds

Plans and packs are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com. For prints, email us.

What happens to my photos?

Exposure asks before your first photo is sent to Google Gemini, the AI that develops your frames. Those photos are not stored on our servers. If you order prints, the frames you chose are held for the print lab and deleted within 21 days. Read the privacy policy for details.
