Exposure Support

Questions, a look that didn't come out right, or a billing problem? Email tarekmoumane@gmail.com and you'll get a reply within two working days.

Common questions

A photo failed. Was I charged?

No. A photo is only counted once the finished image comes back to your phone. If a look fails or you cancel, nothing is taken.

How do daily photos work?

Weekly gives you 5 photos a day and Yearly gives you 10. New photos arrive at midnight, and unused ones bank for up to three days. Photo packs never expire and are used after your daily photos.

I reinstalled the app or got a new phone

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Your plan and any unused packs come back. Your gallery is stored only on your phone, so it is not restored.

How do I cancel?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Exposure. You can also tap Manage subscription inside Exposure's Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What happens to my photos?

Exposure asks before your first photo is sent to Google Gemini, the AI that makes the looks. Photos are processed only to make your image and are not stored on our servers. You can turn this off in Settings at any time. Read the privacy policy for details.
